# Malten strategy

This document records the product and business model behind Malten. It is a
direction for decisions, not a promise that every listed feature exists today.

## Thesis

Malten helps a person build a mental model of their world.

Thoughts, observations, places and moments form a spatial network. AI can work
quietly underneath that network to find relationships and resurface something
when it becomes relevant. It should elevate human attention and understanding,
not turn Malten into another assistant people must talk to.

The valuable moment is not creating the first point. It is when Malten returns
something the person had forgotten and it matters now.

## Open source

Malten is open-source software under the [GNU Affero General Public License
v3.0](LICENSE).

Anyone can inspect it, modify it and run it themselves subject to that license.
Self-hosting is a first-class way to use Malten, not a deliberately degraded
edition or a lead-generation exercise.

The AGPL preserves the same freedom when a modified version is offered over a
network: its corresponding source must also be made available under the
license. It does not prevent Malten from operating a paid hosted service.

The repository contains the software. The commercial product is the dependable
operation of that software and the continuing service around it.

## Commercial model

Malten should be available in three ways:

1. **Self-hosted** — free, open source and operated by the user.
2. **Hosted Free** — the easiest way to begin, with a genuinely useful personal
   mental model and no infrastructure to manage.
3. **Hosted Pro** — a subscription for continuity and compounding value across
   devices, contexts and time.

This is an open-core service model without withholding the core. The free
software must remain capable of capturing, connecting, viewing and exporting a
personal mental model. Pro earns its price by doing costly and operationally
difficult things on the person's behalf.

### Self-hosted

- Complete local network and capture experience.
- Manual connections and place/time context.
- Data export and documented deployment.
- Bring-your-own storage and model credentials where supported.
- No artificial point or usage ceiling imposed by the application.

### Hosted Free

- No account required until a hosted capability needs identity.
- Unlimited local text captures, points and manual connections.
- Basic time and place context.
- Local search and export.
- A modest hosted allowance where server processing is required.

The free tier should allow someone to discover whether Malten belongs in their
life. An arbitrary point limit would make a personal archive feel unsafe and
would charge for storage that costs Malten almost nothing while it remains on
the device.

### Hosted Pro

Initial target price: **£7.99 per month or £69.99 per year**, with a trial once
the Pro experience has enough value to demonstrate.

Pro should eventually include:

- encrypted sync across devices;
- encrypted backup and version history;
- voice, image, link and richer ambient capture;
- automatic semantic connection suggestions;
- contextual resurfacing based on place and time;
- meaning-based search;
- periodic reflection on patterns, unfinished thoughts and emerging themes;
- a reasonable included allowance for model-backed processing.

People should be paying for a personal mental model that stays with them and
grows more useful over time—not for an AI chat box. Privacy, basic export and access to
one's own local network should never be subscription leverage.

Avoid lifetime plans because storage, sync and inference create continuing
costs. Avoid multiple credit tiers, team plans and complex packaging until the
personal product has demonstrated repeat use and willingness to pay.

## Market position

Malten overlaps several existing categories without fitting cleanly into one:

- networked notes such as [Reflect](https://reflect.app/) and
  [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/);
- spatial thinking tools such as [Heptabase](https://heptabase.com/) and
  [TheBrain](https://www.thebrain.com/);
- structured personal knowledge tools such as
  [Capacities](https://capacities.io/);
- ambient private capture such as [mymind](https://mymind.com/).

Those products validate familiar language and willingness to pay. Malten's
distinction is that it begins with lived experience rather than a collection of
documents. Physical and temporal context participate in the network without
turning it into a literal geographic map.

As of September 2026, comparable individual products generally sit between
roughly $8 and $15 per month: Heptabase Pro is $8.99 per month on annual billing,
Capacities Pro is $9.99 monthly or $8.33 on annual billing, Reflect is about
$120 per year, and TheBrain's cloud and AI service is $180 per year. Obsidian
demonstrates the lower-priced service boundary: its unrestricted local product
is free while encrypted Sync is $4 per month annually or $5 monthly.

Malten should initially price below the mature high end while charging enough
to establish that it is a serious personal product.

## Product sequence

Build the paid service only after the product demonstrates its reason to exist:

1. Make capture effortless, especially voice on mobile.
2. Form credible connections without requiring constant manual organisation.
3. Resurface one earlier thought in an appropriate context.
4. Validate that people return and preserve meaningful material.
5. Add identity, encrypted sync, backup and cross-device continuity.
6. Introduce Pro around those hosted capabilities.

The hosted free tier can precede the Pro subscription, but pricing should not
precede value.

## Measures

Early product evidence:

- captures per active person;
- people who create a second session on another day;
- deliberate connections created or accepted;
- resurfaced items opened, kept or dismissed;
- retention after a useful resurfacing event;
- requests for sync or a second device.

Commercial evidence:

- trial starts after experiencing a connection or resurfacing;
- trial-to-paid conversion;
- annual-plan selection;
- retention and voluntary cancellation reasons;
- storage and inference cost per active subscriber.

Raw point counts and time spent staring at the app are not success measures.
Malten should help someone return to their life with a clearer understanding.
